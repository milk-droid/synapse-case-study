The Setup

Researchers work across disciplines, but their tools don't. Synapse is a mobile platform that helps researchers discover, organize, and track academic work - exploring topics, browsing top institutions and labs, managing personalized feeds, and interacting with saved papers and collections.

The Bet

Treat the topic, not the source, as the primary object. Researchers don't think in journals or universities - they think in questions. The IA followed: 26 research topics with subtopics, icons, and a gradient system that signals proximity without locking the taxonomy.

How I Used AI

1

AI for breadth

Used Figma Make to generate multiple UX directions at controlled "temperature" levels. The temperature system: 8-10 = copy closely (low creativity, high fidelity), 5 = balanced (some exploration), 1-3 = freer redesign (high creativity). The first batch was deliberately wide so the right direction would become obvious by exclusion.

2

AI for scaffolding

Used AI-assisted code generation to scaffold reusable components and layouts. Treated the output as a starting point, not an answer.

3

Information architecture, in dialogue

Worked through the 26-topic taxonomy with AI as a thinking partner. Testing hierarchies, naming conventions, how subtopics rolled up. Several IA cuts died in conversation before they ever became frames.

4

Back into Figma

Imported every AI-generated screen into regular Figma to refine spacing, hierarchy, and visual detail. Every interaction and transition added by hand - Figma Make doesn't carry interactions across.

What I Owned

  • All key design decisions - which direction, which hierarchy, which voice
  • The IA - 26 topics with subtopics, icons, gradients, naming, rollup logic
  • Manual refinement of every screen - spacing, hierarchy, type, colour, detail
  • All interactions and transitions - built in Figma after AI screens were imported

Design Decisions

"Topic, not source."

Researchers think in questions; the IA followed.

"Subtopics by gradient."

Visual proximity, not hard categories - the taxonomy stays soft.

"AI proposes, I dispose."

Figma Make's first batch was always too wide; my job was to narrow.

Reflection

AI's job is to widen the field of options. My job is to narrow.

Synapse was the clearest example I've had of this split working. Figma Make produced ten directions; I picked one and refined it. The model scaffolded the components; I owned the spacing, the rhythm, every transition.
